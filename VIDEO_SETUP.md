# Tutorial video

Open `app/lib/tutorial-video.ts` and set `tutorialVideo.youtubeUrl` to your YouTube link. Watch, youtu.be, Shorts, live and embed links are supported. Build and publish again to apply the change.

The card initially displays `poster`, then plays the YouTube embed when clicked. With an empty or invalid YouTube URL it plays the bundled six-second example animation. This is a placeholder, not an instructional tutorial. Update `title` and `poster` for your own video. No video link settings are exposed to visitors.
